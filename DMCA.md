### Requirements for a Valid Notice

To submit a valid DMCA takedown notice, provide a written communication containing the following:

1. **Identification of the copyrighted work**: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyright that is allegedly infringed, along with identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
2. **Identification of the infringing material**: A clear description and exact URL(s) within this repository where the allegedly infringing material is located.
3. **Contact Information**: Your full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
4. **Statement of Good Faith**: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. **Statement of Accuracy**: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

### Notice Submission

Please submit your formal notice by creating an issue labeled `dmca-notice` on this repository, or by contacting the repository maintainers via GitHub with the subject line: `DMCA Takedown Notice - [Repository Name]`.

Upon receipt of a valid and complete notification, the maintainers will review the claim and promptly take down or disable access to the infringing material in accordance with applicable laws.
